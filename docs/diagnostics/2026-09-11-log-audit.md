# Lab error audit — September 11, 2026

Reviewed the retained Lab diagnostic files in the framework checkout and both registered vaults. Window: September 6, 2026 at 18:56 UTC through September 11 at 18:56 UTC, with follow-up checks during repair.

The initial scan found **1,255 ERROR records (34 grouped signatures)** and **46,775 WARNING records**, across 20 current, rotated, and legacy diagnostic log files (about 538 MB). ERROR records were counted from errors.log only, avoiding their duplicate copies in backend/frontend logs. Frontend and backend reports of the same failed request remain separate records; these counts are not counts of independent incidents.
