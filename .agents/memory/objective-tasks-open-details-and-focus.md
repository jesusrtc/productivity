# Objective tasks open details and focus

The user wants task titles to be hyperlinks to their required document/subtab.
Clicking a task opens its details and starts Focus mode. Keep a compact task
control above assets with Off, Semi and Focus: Off restores the usual sidebar;
Semi highlights associated assets in their original order; Focus shows only
task assets, including its required details. Returning to Tasks or choosing an
objective exits focus. Preserve document editing and navigation drafts.

Users choose a task icon from its assets. The existing current workspace tab
shows the task's title and icon while focused, retaining the objective switch
dropdown. A task-linked terminal tab also inherits the icon, without losing its
agent identity, working/completion indicators or fixed launch folder.
