# Objectives open tasks in the working area

In the Objectives demo, the user wants the three focus selectors at the top of
the left column, styled like worktree rows. Clicking a selector opens that
objective's Tasks in the center working area. The sidebar has one Tasks item,
with completed/total progress, rather than individual task items. Tasks and
subtasks are compact single-line rows and must each link to a document, with
document subtabs suitable for their individual details.

The demo counts completed top-level tasks; a parent with subtasks completes
when all of them do. Green means all complete, yellow means on track, orange
means unfinished work is due today or within two days, and red means unfinished
work is past due. Completed work does not contribute to deadline risk. These
are simulation rules; they do not change Assistant task or document behavior.
