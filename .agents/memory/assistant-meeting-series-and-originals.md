# Assistant meetings have explicit series and separate originals

Meetings open on their summary in the existing compact document modal. Lists
and series history use newest-first `yyyy-mm-dd` headers, with unknown dates
last. Keep standalone meetings and explicit workspace-local recurring series.
Raw UTF-8 snapshots are create-only and byte-preserved; supporting legacy notes
are not reconstructed as originals. Questions and generated documents have
separate files and entries in the rail. Fictional data must be labeled Demo.

Entering Meeting notes clears task search/workspace filters. Task URL workspace
parameters never filter meetings, and late task refreshes cannot reapply them.
Explicit meeting filters survive polling. Meeting and series deep links reopen
the corresponding modal; closing cancels pending loads.

Framework storage/commands are documented by `lab context meetings`. Client
ownership, terminology, research and writing policies remain client-owned.
