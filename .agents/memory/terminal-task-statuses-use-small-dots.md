# Terminal task statuses use small dots

The user prefers terminal tabs to look like simple colored-dot/name rows, as in
his October 6, 2026 screenshot. Replace terminal task-status emoji/checkbox
badges with 5px dots, including recommended task terminals and independent
children that inherit a parent's task. Keep status names in hover/accessibility
labels and keep the sidebar's existing status controls. Use filled dots for
In progress/Completed and rings for Not started/Paused; Won’t do is muted.

Assigned worktree names in primary/recommended terminal rows must keep their
matching colored dots and text (all names/colors when multiple are assigned).
Reuse escaped task-name markup; keep canonical task titles and documents.
Avoid duplicate launch-folder captions on primary task rows. Ordinary worktree
terminals show just their checkout's dot/name; asset icons and fixed main icons
remain available. Idle rows have a transparent background/border; hover and
selected rows keep their clear highlight. Preserve all terminal identities,
processes, nesting, WIP filtering and active-Objective visibility.
