# Green terminal dot clears on tab click

The user's latest October 8, 2026 correction replaces timed viewing dismissal:
clicking a terminal tab immediately acknowledges its existing green result,
including an already selected tab. Enter/Space on the tab does the same.
Direct green-dot review remains available. Remove the automatic viewing timer
and its Settings field; old browser delay preferences must not activate it.

Programmatic activation/restoration, hovering, polling, focus changes, time
spent selected and reloads never acknowledge green. If a result finishes while
the tab is selected, it stays green until a later click. A click during work
cannot acknowledge a future result or clear yellow. Merged tabs acknowledge
the actual selected child, while the parent keeps its visual identity.
Double-click continues to open Rename; its first tab click reviews existing green.

The 40-second output quiet period remains the detection rule. This supersedes
timed-viewing rules in older terminal completion memories.
