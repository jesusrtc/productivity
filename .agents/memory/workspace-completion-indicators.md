# Workspace completion indicators

Every open workspace tab shows a blinking green dot while one of its terminals
has unreviewed completed work, even when the workspace is inactive. A batched
background endpoint reads provider activity without capture-pane summaries.
Document-shared terminals count in every workspace where they are visible.

Acknowledgements belong to the exact terminal/conversation across its shared
views, not independently to each workspace. Retain the configurable 20-second
viewing delay and deliberate second-click shortcut. The visible document modal
owns the viewing timer while open; obscured background terminals must not be
acknowledged. The green vertical line remains recency only.
