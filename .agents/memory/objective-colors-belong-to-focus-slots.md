# Objective colors belong to focus slots

Each of the five focus positions always uses its fixed objective color and
reserved four-worktree palette. Colors belong to slots, rather than objective
identity. Moving or inserting an objective recolors it and all shifted
objectives/worktrees to their new positions. Parked objectives are gray.
Extra worktrees must not borrow other slots' reserved colors. Read projections
normalize older registries while preserving their saved order and revision;
mutations persist the new palette without changing object IDs or content.
